# Sourdough brand files

**Feed it and it grows.** The mark is a starter jar: the starter has risen into a dome, well above the gold
band that marks where it began. Bakers put a band on the jar to see whether a starter is alive. Sourdough's
starters are the same: you feed them, and you can see that they grew.

## The mark
The mark is generated by ChatGPT: a starter jar with a gold band, on the Wayne State green `#0C5449` tile.
Nobody draws or edits the mark's geometry. The PNGs below are the mark; every smaller size is a straight
downscale of them.

## Colours
| Role | Colour |
|---|---|
| Tile | Wayne State green `#0C5449` |
| The band (the only gold) | Wayne State gold `#FFCC33` |
| Jar and starter | Cream `#F4F1EA` |
| Site accent only, never in the mark | Crust `#B69018` |

Gold never sits behind white text.

## The board
`sourdough-board.png` is the reference sheet: the hero mark, the app icon, the horizontal and stacked lockups,
the mark on light and dark backgrounds, the colour palette, the typography (Lato Black / Lato Regular), the
favicon previews, the clear space (minimum `x` = a quarter of the icon width), the do's and don'ts, and the
terminal usage. When in doubt about what the mark looks like, look at the board, not at memory.

## Rule
Do not redraw or edit the mark. No new geometry, no "just a little" cleanup, no stretching, no effects. If a
vector is ever needed, trace the PNG with vtracer or potrace, and keep the trace next to the PNG it came
from, never in its place.
